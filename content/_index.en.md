Held since 1985, RPIC brings together researchers, academics, students and industry professionals from Argentina and abroad. It is a forum to present original research results and development experiences, with talks by invited speakers.

The meeting includes the **RPIC Estudiantil** track, where undergraduate students present their research progress. All presentations will be in person.
